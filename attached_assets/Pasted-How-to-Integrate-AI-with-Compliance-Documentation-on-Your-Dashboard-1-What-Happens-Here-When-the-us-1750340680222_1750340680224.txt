How to Integrate AI with Compliance Documentation on Your Dashboard
1. What Happens Here
When the user clicks “Generate Compliance PDF” or “Legal Defense Package”, your app:

Collects all actions, files, audit logs, and evidence for the current project.

Pulls in the relevant protocol excerpts (IMCA, IOGP, HydroDive) you’ve uploaded.

Sends them to the backend for the AI to review and generate a report/check/audit summary.

2. How to “Upload” or Use Docs for the AI
A. Parsing Protocol Docs
You already have uploaded docs (IMCA, HydroDive, IOGP).

If you haven’t yet, extract key sections as text (copy-paste or with a PDF-to-text tool).

Store them in your backend (as JSON, Markdown, or in your DB).

  const prompt = `
You are a compliance AI for offshore safety. 
Protocols: ${JSON.stringify(protocolExcerpts)}
Project: ${JSON.stringify(projectData)}
Audit Trail: ${JSON.stringify(auditLog)}
Files: ${JSON.stringify(files)}

Review everything for this project. 
- Confirm all protocol steps are followed and cite the section for each. 
- Flag any gaps, missing evidence, or non-compliance. 
- Generate a compliance summary (for PDF), an audit report, and a legal defense summary with evidence chain.
  `;

  const response = await openai.createChatCompletion({
    model: "gpt-4o",
    messages: [
      { role: "system", content: "You are a strict safety compliance AI for HydroSafe, always reference protocols." },
      { role: "user", content: prompt }
    ]
  });
  res.json({ aiReport: response.data.choices[0].message.content });
});

5. How the AI “Knows” All the Protocols
By feeding in protocol excerpts from your uploaded docs (as shown above).

The more you extract and organize (“section: text”), the more precise and defensible your AI output will be.

You can expand this over time: each new protocol or standard, just add to your backend doc/DB.
