Step 1: Extract Key Protocols & Procedures for the AI
Goal: Convert your PDF into a structured, machine-readable format so your AI agent can analyze compliance, generate reports, and flag issues in the system.

A. Main Protocol Categories to Extract
Command Roles & Responsibilities
(Bronze – OSC, Silver – EC, Gold – GM; Pages 9–12)

Emergency Response Chain
(Flow, response times, notification procedures)

Medevac & Casevac Procedures
(Primary/secondary routes, decision triggers, response times, contacts; Pages 19–24)

Emergency Scenarios & Response
(Medical, fire, collision, grounding, oil spill, man overboard, security, piracy; Pages 26–36+)

Diving Emergencies & Mitigations
(Loss of comms, air supply, diver recovery, DDC, LARS failure; Pages 47–64)

Contact Details & Assets
(Appendices: all phone numbers, emails, asset readiness, hospitals, clinics, vessel info)

Step 2: Feed This Structured Data to the AI Agent
When a user requests a compliance check or audit, the backend gives the AI all current actions, logs, files, and this protocol JSON.

The AI then checks:

Are all required roles present and assigned?

Were the right contacts notified in the right order?

Did the medevac happen within timelines?

Were all forms and logs completed (with evidence: photos, timestamps)?

Are there missing details, e.g., is an emergency contact out of date, was the backup route verified?

Your job:
- Check if every step in the protocol has been followed, with reference to section (cite exact section or page)
- Flag any missing or overdue actions (e.g., overdue asset check, missing role, delay in medevac)
- Generate a compliance summary (pass/fail for each main category), an audit log, and a list of any recommendations to be fully compliant
Step 4: Connect the App Dashboard
When user clicks “Generate Compliance PDF” or “Export Audit Report”:

The app sends all project logs and structured protocol data to your OpenAI API function

The AI returns a human-readable summary + PDF for download

This output is stored/archived and available for audit and legal defense

Step 5: Continue to Expand
Over time, extract more rules (IMCA, IOGP, custom HydroDive manuals)

Add “lessons learned” and feedback loops from real audits to your database

Use the AI to flag trends (e.g., recurring overdue assets or delays)
