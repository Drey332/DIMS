Instantly visualize asset status (safe, pending, overdue)

Make verification actions and uploads easy and obvious

Clear, modern look with large icons, soft cards, status highlights, and clear dates

Easy audit: every photo/verification is timestamped and linked

Design Details
Color Coding:

Green: Verified/safe asset

Yellow: Pending verification

Red: Overdue/critical asset

Card Shape:

Soft, rounded corners, drop shadow, padding

Content:

Asset name: Bold, large, clear status icon (✔️ ⏳ ⚠️)

Category & location: Smaller, grey text

Verification info: Date, user, and photo evidence

Action buttons:

“Upload Photo” (always available for new verification)

“Mark as Verified” (after upload)

“View Details” (shows photo history, comments, audit log)

“Notify Team” (for overdue assets)

Top Summary Bar:

Quick stats: Verified (green), Pending (yellow), Overdue (red), Total assets (grey/blue)

Export/Audit button always visible

Photo Evidence:

Small camera icon with photo count

Clicking “View Details” opens a modal/lightbox with thumbnails, full-size view, and timestamps

Responsive:

Grid or stack on desktop/tablet; single column on mobile

Style Guide:
Font: Inter, Roboto, or similar clean sans-serif

Icon Set: Material Icons or Heroicons

Buttons: Bold, pill-shaped, blue for actions, red for alerts

Shadows: Soft, for depth (not harsh)

Spacing: 16–24px between cards, 12–16px padding inside

Hover/Focus: Cards lift subtly; buttons change shade
